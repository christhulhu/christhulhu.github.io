This is an overview of present and past projects.

Wildlife Aid (ad hoc)

Over the years, I’ve repeatedly found myself involved in wildlife care — not as a formal project, but as a personal response to immediate needs.

Cause of Divorce (2007–2012)

Grindcore from Aachen

I played bass in the grindcore band Cause of Divorce, during 2010 and 2020. We operated somewhere between DIY ethics, blastbeats, and blown-out rehearsal amps. I played around 100 shows across Germany and neighboring countries, sharing stages with lots of underground acts. I paricipated in a couple of releases, some properly pressed, some handmade by myself.

Ius Talionis (2019–2024)

Black Metal from Aachen

I joined Ius Talionis as a bassist in 2019, at a time when the band was undergoing a significant shift — both stylistically and personally. The earlier roots in traditional black metal gave way to darker, more textured influences from the Icelandic and contemporary black metal scenes.

NecroSlaughter.de (2003–2018)

Underground Metal Zine from the Depths of the Internet

NecroSlaughter.de was a DIY webzine focused on the loud, fast, and ugly corners of extreme metal. Over more than a decade, I published hundreds of reviews, interviews, and scene reports — covering everything from obscure grindcore demos to international black and death metal releases.

TheNocturnalSilence.de (2016–2018)

Everything Gloomy – From Dissonant Death to Dark Alternative

The Nocturnal Silence was a digital zine dedicated to the darker shades of underground music — not limited by genre, but bound by atmosphere. The spectrum ranged from dissonant death metal like Infernal Coil to introspective black metal à la Ultha and haunting dark alternative acts like Chelsea Wolfe.
