**Black Metal from Aachen**

I joined *Ius Talionis* as a bassist in 2019, at a time when the band was undergoing a significant shift — both stylistically and personally. The earlier roots in traditional black metal gave way to darker, more textured influences from the Icelandic and contemporary black metal scenes.

Together, we played several shows across Germany and Belgium, refining our sound and live presence along the way. I contributed to the upcoming album *"Antagonist"*, for which drums and bass were recorded at **Liquid Aether Audio**. Guitars and vocals were tracked independently in DIY fashion, while mix and mastering are once again being handled by Liquid Aether.

Shortly after the recording sessions, I stepped away from the band. *Antagonist* is expected to be released on vinyl later this year.
