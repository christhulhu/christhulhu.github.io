Music plays a major role in my life — not only as a listener, but also as a creator. My taste lives somewhere between dark, atmospheric extremes and classic sonic precision.

But sometimes my musical taste is context dependent. For the gym I prefer the blunt brutality of Death Metal. For relaxation Synthwave offers a nice ambient. And for working in house and garden, my wife and I have a pretty obscure playlist, spanning from Alternative 80s, 90s Euro-Dance and Hip-Hop over Nu-Metal Classics to Speed Metal and Psychobilly. The Spotify algorithms hates us.
