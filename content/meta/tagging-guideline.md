To keep consistency I created this timeline for myself.

- Use lowercase, hyphenated tags (`tag-name`) for consistency.
- Be specific but not overly granular. Combine broader and narrower tags when useful.
- Use nouns over verbs where possible.
- Apply tags based on what the content **is about**, not just what it mentions.
- Tags should reflect purpose or recurring patterns, not just one-off references.
