Integration Project

We began this journey with Arya, who came to us after a rough start in life. She was already with her fourth family in just two years and had never been properly socialized. As a result, she struggles deeply with other cats.

When the twins, Bela and Cooper, joined our home, tensions rose quickly. Since then, we’ve been living with a mesh door separating both parties — a fragile peace maintained through boundaries.

Then came Kalli, a former stray. His arrival unexpectedly shifted the dynamics. Arya and Bela, once like fire and ice, now show occasional signs of cautious tolerance. It’s still a long road ahead, full of slow progress and careful management, but we’re hopeful. One day, we might become a truly unified pride.
