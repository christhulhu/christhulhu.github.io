Welcome! This site is a blend of personal logbook, documentation hub, and knowledge garden.
I write about what I do, build, learn, and sometimes obsess over. Here’s how to get your bearings.

What you’ll find here

  • /about - A short introduction of myself.
  • /now – What I’m currently working on: music, driving license, projects, life.
  • /work - Some background about my profession as Technical Product Manager in R&D.
  • /life - A short summary of my as a person.
  • /pets - An overview on our little zoo.
  • /uses – The gear, software, and workflows I rely on.
  • /colophon – How this site is built, deployed, and maintained.
  • /changelog – A running log of structural and content updates.
  • /notes - Random notes, articles, remarks.

Topics I write about

  • Knowledge & Tools – How I use Obsidian.md, Nextcloud, Markdown, and local-first tools.
  • Tech – Insights from managing complex modernization projects in a corporate context.

What won’t appear Here

  • Anything involving my private life or Hobbies. I simply do not have the time to post everything online - although I make private notes on almost everything. And sometimes privacy is more important than Internet fame.
  • Anything work related. NDAs and discretion are as important as my personal privacy.

What might be here soonish

  • /reading - An excerpt of noteworthy books and articles I’ve read.
  • /listening - Some insights into my musical taste and likes podcasts
  • /uses-not - stuff that I conciously do not use
  • /unicorns - semi-finished or abandoned projects of mine.
